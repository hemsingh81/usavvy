Usavvy PRD — Validation Report

Doc/00-Requirement.md
Poor

This is an unusually disciplined, thesis-driven PRD: it names a real bet ("the board, not the chat"), sequences scope around that bet, quantifies almost every NFR, and is upfront about what it hasn't decided (§22, 8 open questions, an explicit "before engineering commits to estimates" gate). On structure and rhetoric it reads as chain-top-ready — every rubric dimension came back strong or adequate, with zero thin or broken dimensions.

The grade is dragged down by what the adversarial pass found underneath that structure: two concrete, product-critical defects. The mastery/progress formula in §11.2 is internally broken — skipped concepts keep their weight in the denominator while scoring zero in the numerator, permanently capping achievable progress below 100% and contradicting FR-B-11's "excluded from mastery" language, with no defined completion threshold to fall back on. And FR-G-6's anti-stall guarantee — the BA's own words, "not optional... the mitigation that makes this feature safe to ship" against the highest-rated risk in the register (R-4, High/High) — ships with a literal undefined placeholder ("within X days"). Layered on top: a factual contradiction in the launch course count (25 in §3.2 vs. 10 in §7/OQ-4), a forecast algorithm with a unit mismatch, four of the eight "open" stakeholder decisions in §22 that are already answered elsewhere in the same document, and an inconsistently-applied MUST/MVP-blocking convention across Epic 5 and Epic 6 that will mislead whoever authors epics and stories next. None of this is a weak PRD — it's a strong PRD carrying two broken mechanisms and a batch of internal-consistency bugs that a decision-maker should not sign off on as-is.

Substance over theater

Strong

Personas brush against the "more than four" smell but each drives a specific FR, UJ, or NFR rather than sitting decoratively.

Persona count exceeds the theater threshold but is functionally anchored

§4

Strategic coherence

Strong

The thesis is stated and re-stated at the point of maximum leverage (§2, §10, §7's MVP sequencing). The North Star metric measures the thesis directly rather than defaulting to DAU/MAU-style activity, and several metrics carry built-in tension against gaming.

Medium

No explicit counter-metrics named for the North Star or Business Objectives

§20, §3.2
Usavvy has organically embedded tension (checkpoint accuracy bounds, cost-per-learner-hour vs. "explain more" usage) but nothing is labeled as a counter-metric to the North Star or BO-1/BO-2. Without one, a team under growth pressure could inflate mastery counts by softening checkpoints.
Fix: Add an explicit counter-metric row to §20, e.g. "Checkpoint difficulty drift (should not decrease as mastery-concepts/week rises)," or reference §11.2's mastery-weighting as the enforced counter-mechanism.

Done-ness clarity

Adequate

The majority of this PRD is unusually strong on done-ness: most FRs pair with Gherkin acceptance criteria (§8.2, §9.3, §10.5, §11.4), and soft-sounding phrases are consistently operationalized. That said, a handful of MUST-priority items don't carry a testable bound, and one legally sensitive flow is thin.

Medium

FR-G-7 cohort size cap is advisory language under a MUST priority

§12.1
"Cohort size cap for interaction quality: recommend 6–15 learners" is tagged MUST, but "recommend" gives no enforceable bound — an engineer cannot write a test for this as written.
Fix: State a hard bound ("cohort MUST NOT exceed 15; target range 6–15") or re-tag as SHOULD if it's genuinely a guideline.
Medium

NFR-24 fair-use ceiling has no quantified bound

§16.4
"Fair-use ceilings on generation-heavy actions, expressed generously and only enforced against abuse patterns" names a principle but no number and no definition of "abuse pattern," unlike NFR-22/23 in the same section which are precisely measured.
Fix: Specify a placeholder ceiling with an [ASSUMPTION] tag pending the unit-cost model, or explicitly defer the number to that model and say so.
Medium

FR-A-2 minor/parental-consent flow is underspecified for its legal weight

§8.1
The entire flow is one line, with no acceptance criteria (unlike adult onboarding's AC-A-3.1/3.2) and open questions unaddressed: what happens if the parent never responds, how is the parental email verified, what does the minor see while waiting.
Fix: Add acceptance criteria for FR-A-2, or explicitly [NOTE FOR PM] it as deferred to the Data Protection Impact Assessment listed in §1.
Medium

Relationship between onboarding "explanation style default" and "Explanation route" is unstated

FR-A-4 vs. FR-B-21, §10.3
FR-A-4's style vocabulary overlaps with FR-B-21's 8-item route taxonomy (example-first vs. worked example; analogy-first vs. analogy) but nothing states whether onboarding style seeds the first route choice, constrains rotation, or is independent of it.
Fix: Add one sentence to §10.3 or §8.1 clarifying the relationship between the two.

Adversarial review

review-adversarial-general.md

Cynical pass targeting internal consistency, unfulfillable requirements, and self-contradiction rather than structure. 19 findings, none softened.

Critical

Mastery/progress formula is internally broken

§11.2, FR-B-11
Skipped concepts get mastery_score 0.0, but nothing removes their concept_weight from the denominator Σ(concept_weight) — so a skipped concept counts as weight in the denominator and zero in the numerator, permanently capping achievable progress % below 100%. Contradicts FR-B-11's "excluded from mastery." No completion threshold is defined anywhere to resolve what % triggers completion/certificate issuance — this touches BO-2, FR-P-4, and FR-R-5 directly.
Fix: Either exclude skipped concept_weight from the denominator, or define an explicit completion threshold that accounts for it.
Critical

FR-G-6's anti-stall threshold is a literal undefined placeholder

§12.1, R-4
"If a waiting list hasn't formed a cohort within X days" leaves X undefined, even though the accompanying BA note calls this "not optional — it is the mitigation that makes this feature safe to ship" against R-4, itself rated High impact / High likelihood. The one number that operationalizes the product's highest-rated cold-start risk mitigation is missing.
Fix: Set X (even as an [ASSUMPTION]-tagged placeholder pending data) before this reaches architecture.
High

MUST = "MVP-blocking" is violated across Epic 5 and Epic 6

§1, §7, Epic 5, Epic 6
§1's legend defines MUST as MVP-blocking. Epic 5 (Release 3) and Epic 6 (Release 2) tag nearly every requirement plain MUST with no release qualifier (FR-G-1/2/4-7/9-12/16/18, FR-E-1-5/7-14), despite §7 saying those epics ship after MVP. Epic 7/8 do use "(R2)"/"(R3)" suffixes on some items, so the convention exists but is applied inconsistently.
Fix: Apply the release-qualifier convention consistently across every FR table, or state release scope once per epic header instead of per-row.
High

Launch course count contradicts itself

§3.2 BO-5 vs. §7 / OQ-4
BO-5 states "25 catalog courses live at launch," while §7's MVP release plan and OQ-4 both say "10 seed catalog courses." Two different numbers for the same milestone, no reconciling note.
Fix: Reconcile to one number, or clarify that 10 is launch and 25 is a near-term post-launch target distinct from BO-5's "at launch" wording.
High

OQ-6's dependent MUST requirements have no fallback if capacity is unavailable

OQ-6, FR-E-10, FR-E-11
OQ-6 (is human-grading capacity available?) is correctly flagged as blocking, but FR-E-10/FR-E-11 are tagged plain MUST with no conditional fallback. If OQ-6 resolves to "no," these MUST requirements — and the ≥85% agreement-rate success metric that depends on them — are unfulfillable as written, and §7 gives no indication Release 2 scope would shrink.
Fix: State the fallback scope reduction for FR-E-10/11 if OQ-6 resolves negatively.
High

needs_review concept state has no assigned mastery_score

§11.2
The formula's enumerated scores cover in_progress/understood/mastered/skipped only. It's undefined how a decayed (needs_review) concept affects published progress % — meaning a learner's dashboard number can move with no stated rule, in direct tension with Principle 4 ("Honest progress") which this exact section claims to protect.
Fix: Assign needs_review a mastery_score (or an explicit decay function) in the §11.2 formula.
High

Forecast algorithm has a unit mismatch

§11.3
forecast_days = remaining ÷ velocity divides remaining (concept_weight units, per §11.2) by velocity (raw concepts-mastered-per-day). Dimensionally inconsistent for any course with non-uniform concept weights, which §11.2's weighting scheme implies exists.
Fix: Express velocity in weight-per-day, or remaining in raw concept counts — pick one unit system.
High

Beat-caching mitigation doesn't cover the flagship MVP traffic it's meant to make latency targets credible for

R3, NFR-B-1/B-2, J1, FR-C-4
R3 relies on pre-generating and caching Beats for "catalog courses at the standard depth." P1's flagship journey (J1, uploaded content) and FR-C-4 (custom depth/style) both fall outside that cacheable path by construction, and uploaded content is never shared/cached across learners (NFR-14). NFR-B-1/B-2 are nonetheless stated as blanket p90 targets with no segmentation by content type or depth.
Fix: Segment NFR-B-1/B-2 targets by cacheable vs. live-generation paths, or state a separate (looser) target for uploaded/custom-depth sessions.
High

FR-A-8 is tagged SHOULD while NFR-15 asserts GDPR readiness depends on it

FR-A-8, NFR-15
NFR-15 states the product must be "GDPR-ready (data export, erasure, consent records...)" as a compliance scope item, but FR-A-8 (the actual export mechanism) is tagged SHOULD/deferrable. GDPR readiness cannot be claimed without the export function it depends on.
Fix: Promote FR-A-8 to MUST, or scope NFR-15's GDPR-readiness claim to exclude export until it ships.
High

Age-gate verification method and consent sequencing are undefined

FR-A-1, FR-A-2, NFR-16
FR-A-2's age gate is self-declared with no verification method specified, and the sequencing between email verification (FR-A-1, "before first session") and parental-consent gating (NFR-16, "before any account activity") is never defined — leaving it ambiguous whether a self-declared minor can complete verification steps before consent gating triggers. This is exactly the compliance risk area (R-8) the document flags as needing careful handling.
Fix: Define the verification method and the explicit step order between FR-A-1 and the NFR-16 consent gate.
Medium

OQ-2 (market/language) is listed open but already load-bearing elsewhere

OQ-2, §10.6, NFR-15
§10.6's latency targets and §16.3's compliance scope (NFR-15) are already written as settled, explicitly premised on Assumption A1 (India, DPDP Act). The "open" decision has effectively already been answered and built on top of elsewhere in the same draft.
Fix: Either close OQ-2 (promote A1 to a confirmed decision) or explicitly caveat §10.6/§16.3 as provisional on it.
Medium

OQ-3 (Avatar form) is listed open but the architecture is already written around the answer

OQ-3, §19.1, §23
§19.1 makes "a deliberate recommendation" and writes the Voice/Generation architecture around voice-only, and the Glossary flatly defines Avatar as "voice plus presence indicator, not a video character (v1)" as settled fact, not pending.
Fix: Close OQ-3 or caveat §19.1/§23 as provisional pending stakeholder sign-off.
Medium

NFR-23's cost/margin target has a circular dependency on the unresolved price point

NFR-23, OQ-1, §16.4
NFR-23 requires cost-per-learner-hour to "support ≥60% gross margin at the intended price point," but the price point is exactly OQ-1, an unresolved open decision. §16.4's BA note says the unit cost model must be built before pricing is set — creating a circular dependency the document doesn't sequence.
Fix: State the sequencing explicitly (e.g., cost model first with a placeholder margin target, price set second, NFR-23 finalized third).
Medium

No fallback defined once a learner exhausts all explanation routes/examples

FR-B-6, FR-B-21
The route set is finite (eight in FR-B-21) and no fallback behavior is defined for what happens once a learner exhausts all routes and examples for a concept — precisely the struggling-learner scenario Principle 2 is meant to serve.
Fix: Define fallback behavior (e.g., recombine routes, escalate to prerequisite backtrack via FR-B-23, or hand off to human-adjacent support) once the route set is exhausted.
Medium

FR-B-22 is tagged SHOULD but narrated as reliable flagship behavior in J1

FR-B-22, §17 J1
Auto-drop-difficulty-and-reroute is explicitly deferrable per its SHOULD tag, yet the sample journey J1 depicts it as expected, reliable behavior used to narrate the core value proposition to stakeholders reading the journeys.
Fix: Either promote FR-B-22 to MUST for MVP, or rewrite J1 to not depend on deferrable behavior.
Medium

BO-4's 12-month measurement window isn't reconciled with cohorts shipping in Release 3

BO-4, §7, R1
BO-4 ("≥25% of active learners join at least one cohort") is measured over the first 12 months post-launch, but cohorts (Epic 5) are explicitly Release 3, sequenced by the document's own R1 to ship after R1 and R2 are proven out. The effective window to hit 25% could be a small fraction of the stated 12 months.
Fix: Rebase BO-4's window to "first 12 months after Release 3 ships" or state the assumed R1/R2 duration it's measured against.
Low

OQ-5 (peer explain-back) is listed open but already specified

OQ-5, FR-G-13, §12.1, FR-R-1
FR-G-13 already specifies "rotating, opt-in," the §12.1 BA note already recommends "opt-in-per-session with a clear pass," and FR-R-1 already bakes peer-explain-back into the MUST star-award list.
Fix: Close OQ-5 or narrow it to whatever sliver is genuinely still undecided.
Low

OQ-7 (certificate accreditation) is listed open but already answered

OQ-7, FR-R-5
FR-R-5 already mandates the certificate be "explicitly labelled non-accredited" as MUST.
Fix: Close OQ-7 or narrow it to the marketing/legal-review sliver it's actually gating.
Low

Epic 7 leaderboard sub-requirements are scoped inconsistently with their own parent feature

FR-R-7, FR-R-9, FR-R-10, FR-R-6
FR-R-6 (Leaderboards) is correctly tagged "MUST (R2)," but FR-R-7/9/10 — all meaningless without leaderboards existing — are tagged plain MUST with no release qualifier, implying MVP/R1 scope for logic that only matters once the R2 feature ships. Same root cause as the broader MUST/MVP-blocking finding above.
Fix: Tag FR-R-7/9/10 "(R2)" to match FR-R-6.

Mechanical notes

  • Glossary drift: otherwise consistent — "Board," "Beat," "Concept," "Mastery," "Cohort" used identically across FRs, the data model, and UJs. "Learner" vs. "User" is a deliberate distinction (§18), not drift.
  • ID continuity: no gaps or duplicates found in FR-A, FR-B, FR-C, FR-E, FR-G, FR-O, FR-P, FR-R, or NFR-1..24. AC IDs correctly key off their parent FR number. The one legend gap is the undeclared NFR-B-<n> namespace (see Downstream usability).
  • Assumptions Index roundtrip: only A1 (§4) and A2 (§19.1) exist inline, both referenced back in §22 — nothing orphaned, but no dedicated index section.
  • UJ protagonist naming: J1/J2/J3 correctly anchored to named personas; J4 is a floating UJ with no protagonist.
  • Required sections for stakes: for a launch-stakes, chain-top consumer PRD, all expected sections are present — nothing structurally required by the stakes is missing.
